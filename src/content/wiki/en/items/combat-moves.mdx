## How many moves are there?

**75 unique combat moves** at launch, per the official store listing — and none are bought from a shop menu. Every move is discovered and unlocked by playing: progressing your Clan's campaign, training, and reaching rank milestones. Individual move names and exact unlock conditions are being catalogued by the community post-launch (the game released October 23, 2026); this page tracks the verified system and will grow a per-move table as confirmations come in.

![A cat mid-leap trailing light as it executes a special attack](/images/articles/wc-combat-moves.jpg)

## What enemies are moves used against?

The official roster confirms the combat targets your move set needs to cover:

- **Predators** — badgers, vicious foxes, and imposing Dobermanns (dogs), culminating in epic battles
- **Rival Clan cats** — enemy warriors in territory and [skirmish](/guides/game-modes/) battles
- **Prey** — birds, rabbits, and squirrels are tracked and hunted outside of battle

Weakness coverage matters because predator types punish the wrong damage type hard; see [predators and major battles](/bosses/predators-and-battles/) for the fight-by-fight approach.

## What are the move layers?

| Layer | Count | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic techniques | The core of early combat | Available early, scales with training |
| Clan-specific moves | Signature techniques per Clan (ThunderClan, RiverClan, ShadowClan, WindClan) | Campaign progression |
| StarClan ancestor attacks | Rare supernatural finishers | Story beats and rank milestones |

## How do I unlock moves faster?

1. Push your [Clan campaign](/guides/clans-overview/) — campaign progress gates Clan-specific moves.
2. Train consistently — level and training raise the base stats that every move scales from.
3. Fight everything — predator and [rival Clan](/bosses/rival-clan-battles/) victories feed the unlock track.
4. Climb ranks — the rank ladder (kit to legend) is tied to the strongest unlocks.

## How should I choose which moves to use?

Match your move set to what you fight. Weakness coverage beats raw power — a move hitting a [predator's weakness](/bosses/predators-and-battles/) outperforms a nominally stronger move into resistance. Keep one direct technique for normal turns, one Clan signature for break windows, and one ancestor attack for emergencies. See the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/) for the full timing-and-weakness model.

<Callout type="info" title="Procedural generation changes the grind">The official listing states that characters, quests, and stories are procedurally generated — no two playthroughs are the same. In practice that means move-unlock pacing and which battles you meet first vary between cats and Clans. The 75-move total is fixed; the road to completing your collection isn't.</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Are moves shared between Clans?">Basic techniques are shared; Clan-specific moves and ancestor attacks are locked to your Clan's identity. Multi-Clan playthroughs are the only way to see the full 75.</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I re-spec my move set?">You assemble your available moves per battle from everything you have unlocked — experimentation is free, unlocks are permanent.</Accordion>
