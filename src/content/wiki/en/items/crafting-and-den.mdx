## How does crafting work?

Explore the Lake Territories and forage the land for materials, then return to your den to craft. The official feature set calls out bedding and scratching posts as craftable staples — items that are both decoration and a reflection of your cat's personality. Crafting turns exploration loot into a home worth visiting.

![A cozy den interior with moss bedding, a scratching post, and collected trinkets](/images/articles/wc-crafting-den.jpg)

## What can I craft?

At launch the craftable catalog centers on den life:

- **Bedding** — your cat's sleeping spot, with cosmetic variants.
- **Scratching posts** — functional-flavored decor for the den.
- **Decorations** — unlockable items earned through play that personalize the space.

New craftables unlock as you progress your campaign and reputation, so your den grows alongside your legend.

## Where do materials come from?

Materials are foraged out in the world — gathered while you travel between quests across the four Clans' territories (forest, marshlands, pine swamps, and moors; see the [clans overview](/guides/clans-overview/) for each region's identity). If you enjoy the collection loop, plan route passes through landmark-dense areas between story beats rather than dedicated farming trips.

## Why customize my den?

Two reasons. First, progression: your den is the visible record of your journey. Second, community: asynchronous online den visits let friends drop into your den and see your cat and decoration work — the game's main way to show off your [cat creator](/guides/cat-creator/) design. Visiting others' dens is also a good source of ideas for your own layout.

<Callout type="tip">The Purrfect Look Pack (formerly a pre-order bonus, now sold as a separate DLC) adds extra cosmetic options — some may apply to den items as well as your cat. Check the in-game customization menu if you own it.</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Does den customization affect stats?">No — it's cosmetic. Power comes from training and your [combat move unlocks](/items/combat-moves/); the den is your trophy room.</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can other players visit my den?">Yes, asynchronously — friends can visit your den online without you needing to be online at the same time.</Accordion>
