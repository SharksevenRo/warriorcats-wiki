## What modes are in the game?

Three, and they serve different moods. **Prophecy Mode** is the single-player heart — four complete story campaigns, one per Clan. **Defender Mode** is a score-attack survival run against the game's fiercest enemies. **Skirmish Mode** is the multiplayer option: asynchronous 3v3 battles against other players.

![A warrior cat across three scenes: story hunt, camp defense, and skirmish](/images/articles/wc-game-modes.jpg)

## What is Prophecy Mode?

The main campaign. You create a cat, join one of the four Clans, and play that Clan's original story — its prophecies, rivalries, and challenges shaped by its culture. Four campaigns means four distinct perspectives on the same Lake Territories, which is where most of the game's [move unlocks](/items/combat-moves/) and rank progression happen. Quests, characters, and stories are procedurally generated, so a second cat's campaign won't repeat the first one's beats.

## What is Defender Mode?

A single-run gauntlet: face all of the game's fiercest enemies in sequence and see how long you last. It is the purest test of the [combat system](/guides/combat-system/) — no story padding, just your build against escalating [predators and battles](/bosses/predators-and-battles/). Expect it to expose gaps in your move coverage fast.

## What is Skirmish Mode?

Asynchronous online battles: your three-cat party against another player's, resolved without both players online at once. It uses the same 3v3 turn-based rules as [rival Clan battles](/bosses/rival-clan-battles/) — think of it as the PvP ladder. Your den visits also run through this online layer, letting friends see your [cat and den](/items/crafting-and-den/) whenever they drop by.

## Which mode should I play first?

Prophecy Mode — it teaches the systems, unlocks your kit, and builds the character you will take everywhere else. Add Defender Mode runs when you want a quick combat fix, and dip into Skirmish once your move set covers at least two elements.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Is there co-op or real-time PvP?">No real-time multiplayer at launch — Skirmish battles are asynchronous and den visits are passive.</Accordion>
