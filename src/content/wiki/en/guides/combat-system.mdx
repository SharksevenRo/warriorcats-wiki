## How does combat work?

Battles are turn-based with up to three cats per side. On your turn you pick actions from your unlocked move list — but timing matters. Well-timed inputs strengthen your attacks and defenses, so combat rewards rhythm and anticipation more than raw menu play. Target enemy weaknesses to break their guard faster.

![Three cats brace for battle against a looming enemy, elemental energy swirling](/images/articles/wc-combat-system.jpg)

## What are the 75 combat moves?

The game ships 75 unique moves, all discovered and unlocked through play rather than bought from a list. They fall into three layers:

| Layer | What it does | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic techniques | Standard strikes with Clan flavor | Available early, improves with training |
| Clan-specific moves | Signature techniques reflecting your Clan's identity | Unlocked by progressing your campaign |
| StarClan ancestor attacks | Powerful supernatural strikes granted by StarClan | Earned through key story and rank milestones |

The full [combat move list](/items/combat-moves/) tracks every move and how it is unlocked.

## How do weaknesses work?

Enemies carry elemental and behavioral weaknesses. Hitting a weakness deals bonus damage and builds toward breaking the enemy's stance — once broken, they take heavily increased damage for a window. This is the core skill check against [predators and major battles](/bosses/predators-and-battles/): bring coverage for the enemy's weakness element before the fight, not during it.

## What are StarClan ancestor attacks?

StarClan-granted attacks are your big red button. These ancestor techniques hit far harder than normal moves and can swing a losing 3v3. They are earned, not spammed — expect to unlock them at rank milestones and story beats, and to use them a handful of times per battle at most.

## How should I build my move set?

1. Cover at least two elements so you can exploit weaknesses (see [predator guide](/bosses/predators-and-battles/)).
2. Keep one reliable direct technique for normal turns.
3. Save your Clan signature + ancestor attack for break windows.
4. Re-visit your loadout as new moves unlock — 75 moves means your kit evolves constantly.
