## How many editions are there?

Two at launch, per the official release information: the **Standard Edition** and the **Feral Edition**. Both are rated **E10+** and both are available on PC (Steam and Epic), PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2 — pick your edition on your platform of choice, not the other way around.

![Two warrior cats face off across a misty clearing at dawn](/images/articles/wc-editions.jpg)

## Standard vs Feral at a glance

| | Standard Edition | Feral Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $29.99 / £24.99 | $39.99 / £34.99 |
| Full game | Yes | Yes |
| Extra creation elements | — | 8 |
| Accessories | — | 12 |
| Cel-shaded render style | — | Yes |
| Photo mode extras | — | 4 poses + 4 effects |

The Feral Edition is a cosmetics bundle — nothing in it changes combat stats or campaign progress. If you're here for the stories and turn-based battles, Standard carries the complete game. If your cat's look is half the fun (and in a game with this much [character creation](/guides/cat-creator/), it might be), the Feral pack is the one.

<Callout type="info">The cel-shaded render style applies a comic-book look to your cat — a natural fit for screenshots, which is exactly what the bundled photo mode poses and effects are for.</Callout>

## What is the Purrfect Look Pack?

Pre-ordering **any edition** granted the **Purrfect Look Pack** at launch. It has since appeared as a separate, purchasable DLC on Steam, so latecomers aren't locked out of the extra looks — check the game's DLC list on the store page if you want it.

## Which should you buy?

- **Buy Standard** if you want the four Clan campaigns, [game modes](/guides/game-modes/), and 3v3 combat without extras.
- **Buy Feral** if you'll spend real time in the creator and photo mode, or you simply want the most complete cosmetic kit.

Either way, read the [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/) before your first session — your Clan choice matters more than your edition choice.
