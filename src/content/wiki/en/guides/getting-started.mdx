## What do I do first in Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest?

Start in the cat creator: pick your Clan's look with fur patterns, colors, markings, ears, eyes, and whiskers, then name your cat and set its nature. You always play as a warrior — other roles from the books (medicine cat, etc.) are not playable. After creation you enter your Clan's territory and begin its unique story campaign.

![A young warrior cat looks out over the misty forest territories at dawn](/images/articles/wc-getting-started.jpg)

<Callout type="tip">Your first choice matters more than your stats: each of the four Clans has its own campaign, culture, and combat techniques. See the [clans overview](/guides/clans-overview/) before committing.</Callout>

## Which Clan should I pick?

Pick the territory and fighting style that fits you — the choice locks in your campaign:

| Clan | Territory | Fighting identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ThunderClan | Dense forest | Bold, direct attacks |
| RiverClan | Marshlands | Flowing, adaptive techniques |
| ShadowClan | Dark pine swamps | Stealth and deception |
| WindClan | Open moors | Speed and agility |

Every Clan campaign covers the same Lake Territories from a different point of view, with different prophecies, rivals, and challenges.

## How does the core gameplay loop work?

The loop is quest → hunt/fight → earn reputation → rank up:

1. Take quests around your camp and territory to gain favor with your Clan.
2. Hunt prey (mice, squirrels, and more) and fight off predators to train your cat.
3. Win [turn-based battles](/guides/combat-system/) — 3v3 fights with timing mechanics.
4. Rise through the ranks and unlock new [combat moves](/items/combat-moves/) as your legend grows.

## What are the game modes?

Three modes ship at launch (version 1.0): **Prophecy Mode** — four full story campaigns, one per Clan; **Defender Mode** — a single-run survival gauntlet against the game's fiercest enemies; and **Skirmish Mode** — asynchronous online battles against other players' cats.

## How do I get stronger?

Level and training raise your base stats (which depend on your Clan), while the 75 unique [combat moves](/items/combat-moves/) are discovered and unlocked through play. StarClan-granted ancestor attacks add a supernatural layer for big fights. For the toughest encounters, read our [predators and boss battles](/bosses/predators-and-battles/) guide before you engage.

## Is there multiplayer?

There is no real-time co-op campaign. Multiplayer is asynchronous: in Skirmish Mode you battle other players online, and you can visit friends' dens asynchronously to show off your customized cat and decorations.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Can I play a medicine cat or leader?">No — you are locked into the warrior role. The game is built around the warrior's journey from newcomer to legend, with rank ceremonies marking your progress.</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the game follow the books' story?">The campaigns are original stories set in the book universe's Lake Territories, featuring both original characters and iconic cats from the series.</Accordion>
