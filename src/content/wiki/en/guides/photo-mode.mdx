## What does the Feral Edition add visually?

Per the official release information, the **Feral Edition** bundles a **photo mode with 4 poses and 4 effects**, alongside a **cel-shaded render style** that gives the game a comic-book look. These sit on top of the extra creation elements and 12 accessories — together they're the edition's whole pitch: this is a game about *your* cat, and Feral is the toolkit for showing that cat off.

![A customized warrior cat poses on a mossy log, rendered in the Feral Edition's cel-shaded style](/images/articles/wc-photo-mode.jpg)

## How do I get the most out of photo mode?

1. **Build the cat first.** Spend real time in the [cat creator](/guides/cat-creator/) — the poses and effects only shine when the subject is yours.
2. **Scout a backdrop.** The four Clan territories give you four distinct biomes: dense forest, open moor, reed marsh, and dark pine swamp. The [Lake Territories map](/maps/lake-territories/) is your location scout.
3. **Match effect to biome.** A mist effect in ShadowClan's pines reads very differently from the same effect on WindClan's bright moor — take one shot per territory and compare.
4. **Dress the set.** If you've invested in [den crafting](/items/crafting-and-den/), your camp doubles as a studio.

## Is photo mode worth the upgrade?

If you mainly want the campaigns and combat, it isn't — the [editions guide](/guides/editions-guide/) lays out what Standard already covers. But if half your play session ends with "let me get one more shot of her by the lake," the Feral Edition is aimed squarely at you.

<Callout type="info">The Purrfect Look Pack adds even more creation options, and it's available as a separate DLC on Steam — extra looks don't require buying the whole Feral Edition again.</Callout>
