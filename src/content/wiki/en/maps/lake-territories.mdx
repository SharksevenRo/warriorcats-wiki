## What are the Lake Territories?

The game is set in the book universe's **Lake Territories** — the same forest, marsh, moor, and pine landscape readers know, rebuilt as one explorable game map. The four Clans each claim a corner of it, and every Clan campaign walks the same ground from a different point of view, with its own story written with series author Erin Hunter's involvement.

![A warrior cat pads along the lakeshore between pine and open moor](/images/articles/wc-lake-territories.jpg)

## The four Clan territories

Each Clan's homeland matches its character, as covered in the [clans overview](/guides/clans-overview/):

| Clan | Territory |
| --- | --- |
| ThunderClan | Dense deciduous forest |
| RiverClan | Marshlands and reeds around the water |
| ShadowClan | Dark pine swamps |
| WindClan | Open, windswept moors |

Picking a Clan is picking a starting map biome as much as a story — a WindClan cat and a RiverClan cat will describe "the forest" very differently.

## How does exploration work?

Four systems drive travel through the territories:

1. **Scent tracking** — follow smells through the undergrowth to locate prey, paths, and points of interest, the way a warrior actually reads the forest.
2. **Climbing** — vertical routes open up shortcuts and vantage points a ground-only run would miss.
3. **Hunting** — prey animals roam the territories; stalking and catching them feeds your training loop.
4. **Environmental puzzles** — the landscape itself gates progress, asking you to read terrain rather than menus.

<Callout type="tip">Gather as you go. Materials collected in the territories feed the den decoration system — the [crafting and den guide](/items/crafting-and-den/) covers what to keep and what to skip.</Callout>
