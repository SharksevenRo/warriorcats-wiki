## How do rival Clan battles work?

Each enemy Clan's war party fights the way that Clan is designed to: ThunderClan pressures directly, RiverClan adapts mid-fight, ShadowClan strikes from stealth, and WindClan tries to act first and end the fight early. The AI fights by the same stance rules you do, so expect well-set-up combinations if you let a fight drag.

![Two groups of clan cats face off across a riverbank](/images/articles/wc-bosses-rival.jpg)

## How do I counter each Clan style?

| Enemy Clan | Their plan | Your counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ThunderClan | Direct pressure, heavy hits | Out-sustain: defend timed turns, punish whiffed big attacks |
| RiverClan | Adaptive technique swaps | Commit hard to one element they can't cover |
| ShadowClan | Stealth strikes, debuffs | Speed and detection — never let them control the tempo |
| WindClan | Act first, rush down | Bulk and break-windows — survive the opener, then burst |

## What party setup wins most fights?

Build around coverage and focus fire:

1. Cover at least two elements across your three cats so every enemy weakness is hittable (see the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/)).
2. Focus one enemy at a time — a 3v2 is far easier to control than three 1v1s.
3. Keep one defensive or sustain option in your move set; Clan battles reward the side that still has resources in round three.
4. Spend [StarClan ancestor attacks](/items/combat-moves/) to delete the enemy's biggest threat, not to pad damage on a nearly-dead target.

## Where do rival Clan battles happen?

They appear throughout the four Prophecy Mode campaigns as territory and story conflicts, and Skirmish Mode extends the same 3v3 rules to asynchronous battles against other players' cats — your best practice ground, since human move sets are meaner than the AI's.

<Callout type="tip">Playing more than one [Clan campaign](/guides/clans-overview/) is the fastest way to learn every style from the inside — you will know exactly what a WindClan opener wants because you have played it.</Callout>
