## What are the major battles?

Encounters escalate from hunting prey (mice, squirrels) to fighting off predators and, ultimately, rival Clan warriors and story bosses. Predators — badgers, dogs, and snakes — hit harder and take more punishment than any prey animal, and story battles against other Clans act as set-piece milestones in each campaign.

## How do I beat predators?

Predators are damage checks with a weakness puzzle on top:

1. **Scout before engaging.** Enter with a move set that covers the predator's weakness element — see the [combat system guide](/guides/combat-system/) for how weaknesses break stances.
2. **Time your inputs.** Well-timed actions power up attacks; sloppy timing drags the fight and lets the predator's bigger turns come around more often.
3. **Break, then burst.** Hit the weakness until the predator's guard breaks, then unload your Clan's signature technique and a [StarClan ancestor attack](/items/combat-moves/) inside the break window.

<Callout type="danger" title="Don't fight above your rank">Predators roaming later territories assume a trained party. If a fight is unwinnable, quest and hunt in your current area first — level and training raise your base stats substantially.</Callout>

## How do rival Clan battles work?

Clan battles are 3v3 against organized opponents with their own techniques — the AI plays by the same timing and weakness rules you do. Expect them to punish a one-element move set: a party that only knows direct strikes will get stalled and out-sustained. Bring coverage, protect your weakest cat, and focus one enemy at a time.

## What makes boss battles different?

Story bosses layer mechanics onto the standard fight: adds that must be cleared first, attacks that demand specific responses, or phases that change the weakness table. The shared skeleton stays constant — break the stance, burst in the window, keep your heaviest [moves](/items/combat-moves/) for the final phase. Defender Mode's single-run gauntlet is the purest test of these skills: every predator and major enemy type, back to back.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Which Clan is best for hard fights?">Whichever you've invested in — power comes from your unlocked move list and training, not Clan choice. That said, WindClan's speed and ShadowClan's stealth give more room for error against fast or punishing bosses.</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I retry a lost boss fight?">Yes — losing a battle returns you to the state before it. Rebuild your move set and try a different element spread.</Accordion>
